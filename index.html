— Our Philosophy

Made with care. Served with character.

We are a small, mobile pastry kitchen built on the belief that a great coffee break can change the rhythm of your day. Every cup is pulled by hand. Every pastry is baked the morning of. Nothing mass-produced, nothing rushed.

— The Signature

A short menu.
Done exceptionally.

We keep it focused — a tightly edited menu that lets every ingredient shine. Pull up to the window, order at the hatch, and taste why Lakeland can't stop talking about us.

01
Coffee · Signature

Greca Cortadito

A Mediterranean-style double espresso, lightly cut with sweet condensed milk. Our namesake serve.

02
Pastry · Signature

Pistachio Tres Leches

Three-milk sponge soaked overnight, crowned with whipped mascarpone and crushed Bronte pistachios.

03
Coffee · Latte

Honey Lavender Latte

Steamed milk, raw wildflower honey, a whisper of dried Provençal lavender, finished with latte art.

04
Breakfast

Almond Croissant

72-hour laminated dough, double-baked with frangipane and toasted Marcona almonds.

05
Cake · Daily Bake

Olive Oil Citrus Cake

Single-origin olive oil, Sicilian lemon zest, candied orange, finished with sea salt.

06
Matcha · Speciality

Ceremonial Matcha Latte

Stone-milled Uji matcha whisked with oat or whole milk. Clean, vegetal, gently sweet.
